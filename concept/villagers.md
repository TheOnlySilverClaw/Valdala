# Villagers

Villagers are the primary inhabitants of settlements. They build houses and other constructions, craft items, transport goods and perform all kinds of work.

Villagers have their own agenda and cannot be directly controlled by players. However, when players maintain a good relationship with a settlement, its villagers become more willing to accept requests to perform tasks.

To work with villagers, it is important to keep their morale and esteem high.


## Morale

Morale is a summarized measurement of an individual villager's happiness and willingness to perform work. Villagers with high morale tend to perform tasks more effectively and for extended periods of time, whereas those with low morale perform worse and occasionally abandon their tasks entirely.

Morale depends on a villager's needs being fulfilled:

### Water

Water is replenished by drinking from a natural water source or a drink container.
Water depletes faster at higher temperature and while doing physically demanding tasks.

### Food

Food depletes constantly and additional food is required by doing physically demanding tasks.
Food is replenished by eating and the amount depends on the food source.

### Sleep

Sleep is replenished by sleeping at least one ingame hour without interruption. High quality beds, pleasant room temperature and safety of the settlement improve the amount of sleep recovered per hour.
Sleep constantly depletes over time while not sleeping.

### Rest

Rest is used up by working tasks and different tasks deplete rest faster than others.
Rest can be recovered simply by not performing any tasks that deplete it. Rest is recovered faster when done on furniture like benches or chairs.

### Company

Company slowly depletes when villagers are too far away from any entity they consider friendly and recovers when close enough.
Friendly entities include villagers of their own or allied settlements, certain domestic animals and players with a friendly relationship.

### Comfort

Comfort slowly improves while the environment around the villager

- is warm enough
- is not too warm
- is not exposed to rain
- allows for free movement

and depletes otherwise.
