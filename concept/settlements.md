# Settlements

Settlements are designated locations in the world that allow for the construction of advanced buildings, production of ressources and housing of players, villagers, domestic animals and other inhabitants.

Settlements appear on their own in suitable and unclaimed locations of the world.

Players can establish their influence over existing settlements or found their own by planting flags and attracting inhabitants.


## Attraction

Attraction increases the influx of new villagers into a settlement. Attraction can be negative, in which case villagers will start leaving.

Attraction is derived from multiple factors:

### Morale

the average morale of villagers currently living in a settlement

### Size

the number of villagers currently living in a settlement

### Capacity

the amount of housing space that is currently unclaimed

### Safety

the frequency, amount and might of hostile creatures being detected around the settlement


## Esteem

Esteem measures an individual player's standing with a whole settlement. Generally, a player starts out with 0 esteem from each contacted settlement, which means the villagers will be neutral and cautious towards them.

Esteem can be increased by helping a settlement and decreases by ignoring its needs or actively harming it.

Esteem is affected by the following actions:


### Tribute

Tributes are ressources that are given without a direct compensation.

A player can give ressources as a tribute to increase the settlement's esteem.

A player can demand ressources as a tribute from a settlement. Depending on their esteem and availability, the tribute may be granted. Regardless of the outcome, each request decreases esteem.


### Trade

Trade is the exchange of ressources that are oversupplied in one location and in demand in another.

Trade can happen between settlements, players, independent merchants and other entities.

Depending on the value and volume of trade, the relationship between trading partners slowly increases.


### Hostility

Damaging villagers, animals, buildings, food and water sources or any other entity or structure related to a settlement greatly decreases esteem.


### Protection

Attacking and killing creatures that are hostile towards a settlement greatly increases esteem.
